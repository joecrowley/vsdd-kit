## OpenSpec

- **Project:** [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec) (`@fission-ai/openspec`)
- **What the kit adapts:** OpenSpec's `spec-driven` schema. The kit's
  `files/openspec/schemas/visual-driven/schema.yaml` and its templates
  (`templates/proposal.md`, `spec.md`, `design.md`, `tasks.md`) are based on it. The kit
  adds the `diagrams` artifact, and changes the instructions and templates. The
  installer copies these files into each project's `openspec/schemas/visual-driven/`,
  and `schema.yaml` carries a short notice pointing here.
